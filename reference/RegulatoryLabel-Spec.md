# TRANCHE Regulatory Label Specification

Status: content and nutrition work in progress; printer dimensions and compliance review pending

## Pilot format

- Product: Classic Table White
- Finished size: 100 x 150 mm, portrait (provisional until the bread bag is selected)
- Provisional outer bag: 220 mm wide x 280 mm high with a 110 mm base; confirm the flat usable rear panel and closure folds from a physical sample
- Artwork: vector SVG master plus 300 DPI PNG export
- Background: warm white
- Text: dark brown/near-black for strong contrast
- Accent: muted olive, used sparingly
- Variable entries: handwritten or stamped on a matte, writable surface
- Safe margin: 4 mm minimum inside the trim; confirm the printer's tolerance before reducing it further
- No bleed assumed for the first draft; confirm with the label printer

Printer status as of 21 July 2026: the 100 x 150 mm finished size, bleed and safe-area requirements have not yet been confirmed by the printer. Continue using the current format for content proofs only.

### Fixed product information

INGREDIENTS

Wheat flour blend (refined wheat flour, whole wheat flour and vital wheat gluten), filtered water, salt and instant yeast.

Ingredients appear by name only and in descending order by recipe weight. Recipe percentages and quantities are not printed in the ingredient declaration.

Confirmed declaration policy: declare each MFC master flour as a compound ingredient, for example `wheat flour blend (refined wheat flour, whole wheat flour, vital wheat gluten)`, rather than scattering its components through the ingredient list. Order the flour blend by its total recipe allocation and order its components by their weight within the blend.

ALLERGEN INFORMATION

Contains wheat and gluten.

Made in a kitchen that also handles milk and milk products, tree nuts, soy, sesame and other seeds.

Note: Review the cross-contact statement against actual kitchen ingredients and controls. Do not include an allergen merely as a generic precaution.

### Variable batch block

The following fields remain blank on the product-specific preprinted label:

- Batch/Lot No.: ____________________
- Packed on: ________________________
- Best before: ______________________
- Net Quantity: __________________ g
- MRP: Rs. __________ (inclusive of all taxes)

The actual net quantity at packing must not be lower than the declared quantity. Confirm the production and compliance policy before using variable loaf weights.

Keep Packed on, Best before and Net Quantity on this outer retail-facing label even when the inner freshness seal repeats them. The declarations should be readily visible to the customer without opening or removing the outer bag. The inner copy is a convenience and traceability duplicate after the paper bag is discarded; both copies must match. MRP remains on the outer regulatory label.

### Universal inner freshness seal

Use one 50 x 150 mm writable seal across all bread products. The seal carries Product, Packed on and Net weight fields. These values must match the corresponding declarations on the outer regulatory label. Best before, Batch/Lot No. and MRP remain on the outer label and are not duplicated on the seal.

### Threaded bread-care and thank-you card

Use one double-sided 65 x 90 mm portrait card with a centred thread hole. The thank-you side uses the circular Tranche logo, the approved small-bakery message and a feedback invitation directing customers to message or tag `@tranchebakery`. The reverse pairs five bread-care instructions with small line icons and includes the only QR code on the card, linking to `tranchebakery.in/faq.html#storage-guide`. Keep `Logo.PNG` and `BreadCare-StorageQR.png` beside the editable SVG files when sending the artwork to print.

## Product-family workflow

Use this compact pilot as the structural master. Each product receives its own fixed product name, description, ingredients, allergens and nutrition values. Keep the typography, nutrition grid, business block and handwritten variable block consistent, but allow the ingredient and allergen zones to reflow vertically for longer declarations. Do not shrink mandatory copy merely to force identical coordinates across every product.
