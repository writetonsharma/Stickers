## Calculation Decisions

- MFC allocations are expanded as finished master-blend weights, including vital wheat gluten in the recorded master total.
- The 50:50 olive mix is measured after both olive types are drained and rinsed. Supplier-panel sodium is retained as a conservative proxy.
- Caramelized-onion preparation is allocated as 55 parts onion to 10 parts absorbed olive oil.
- Potato is plain cooked potato.
- Garlic-herb knots include 24 g retained glaze per batch: 19.2 g butter and 4.8 g olive oil; garlic and herbs are trace.
- Each sweet bun uses a 150 g pre-bake portion, 10% bake loss and 5 g prepared 1:1 sugar syrup, giving an estimated 840 g six-piece pack.
- Vanilla was removed from Nordic Cardamom Buns.
- Malt and baking-soda bath uptake is treated as nutritionally negligible.
- Multi-Seed Loaf uses 385 g total process water; water does not change listed nutrient totals.
- Focaccia uses the recorded approximate 495 g baked yield for the 8×8 inch tray.
